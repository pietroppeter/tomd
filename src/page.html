tomd

Paste a page URL, get it back as Markdown. Supported for now: Nim forum threads (forum.nim-lang.org) and Google Scholar profiles (scholar.google.com/citations?user=…).

 
Also works by putting this site's address in front of a URL: /https://forum.nim-lang.org/t/12345 (or /md?url=…). Source: pietroppeter/tomd.
